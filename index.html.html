Purpose and method

Evidence workbook behind the consolidated Top 10 batters and bowlers. Format-level final scores are combined using Medium + High pressure sample balls as weights (batters: actual balls; bowlers: actual legal balls).

The result is a sample-weighted average of format-normalised scores, not a fresh score from every raw delivery.

Reliability adjustment: 50 + (raw score − 50) × reliability. Medium and High pressure weights are 0.50 and 1.00; Low pressure is excluded.

Bowler economy percentile was recalculated within each format, so lower economy scores higher.

Cautions

  • Pressure average can be undefined when a player has no pressure dismissal, so read it alongside runs, balls, strike rate and sample size.
  • Pressure matches and opponent counts come from format-level records, and opponent counts are the format-level maximum rather than a deduplicated union.
  • Best-match share is the maximum format-level share, a warning indicator only.
  • Rankings are model outputs based on the supplied data, thresholds, eligibility filters, weights and sample rules, not definitive measures of ability.

Final scores are sample-weighted averages of format-normalised, reliability-adjusted scores, so treat them as model outputs, not definitive measures of ability.
